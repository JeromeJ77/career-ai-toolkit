# Senior Software Engineer, Core Ledger — Northwind Ledger

*Fictional job posting · Remote (EU) with quarterly on-sites in Amsterdam · Full-time*

Posted 25 September 2026 · Job ID: NWL-ENG-2026-087

## About Northwind Ledger

Northwind Ledger is a fictional Series B scale-up (210 people, 14 nationalities) building the accounting and treasury backbone for mid-sized European e-commerce companies. Our ledger processes 40 million entries a month and must be correct to the cent, every time. Engineering is fully remote across the EU, with English as the working language.

## The role

You will join the **Core Ledger** team (8 engineers), the team that owns the double-entry ledger at the heart of the product. This is a hands-on senior role with real architectural influence: you will design, build and operate services that every other team depends on.

### What you will do

- Design and implement ledger services in **Kotlin and Java on the JVM** (Spring Boot), backed by PostgreSQL and an event log on Kafka.
- Own correctness: idempotent writes, exactly-once semantics where they matter, reconciliation jobs, and the tests that prove them.
- Drive technical decisions across teams through RFCs and design reviews.
- Mentor engineers and raise the bar on code review and operational excellence.
- Share on-call for the ledger (one week in eight, compensated), and lead blameless post-mortems.
- Help us scale to 200 million entries a month without rewriting everything.

### What we are looking for

- 6+ years of backend engineering, with deep JVM experience. Kotlin is a plus, not a requirement; we expect you to pick it up quickly.
- Strong distributed-systems fundamentals: consistency models, partitioning, failure handling.
- Track record of running systems in production and improving their reliability.
- Experience influencing architecture beyond your own team.
- Clear written and spoken English. You will write RFCs and present them.
- Based in the EU, with the right to work there.

### Nice to have

- Fintech, payments or accounting domain knowledge.
- Experience with contract testing or schema governance across many teams.
- Public speaking or writing about engineering topics.

## Compensation and benefits

- €75,000 – €90,000 base, depending on location and experience, plus equity.
- Fully remote within the EU; four on-site weeks a year in Amsterdam (travel covered).
- €3,000 yearly learning budget, home-office stipend, 30 days of paid leave.

## Interview process

1. **Recruiter call** (30 min) — motivation, logistics, compensation expectations.
2. **Hiring manager interview** (45 min) — your experience and how you work.
3. **System design interview** (60 min) — you will design a part of a ledger-like system on a shared whiteboard with two engineers.
4. **Technical deep dive** (60 min) — a detailed conversation about a system you built, with a focus on trade-offs and incidents.
5. **Values interview** (45 min) — with two people from other teams.

We aim to complete the process within four weeks.
