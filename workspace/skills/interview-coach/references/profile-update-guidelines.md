# Controlled profile updates

Never update the profile silently.

Present:

- additions proposed;
- enrichments proposed;
- replacements proposed, with old and new wording;
- removals or archival suggestions;
- facts to confirm;
- opportunity-specific content deliberately not capitalized;
- the effect on the profile status and version (for example « version 0.2 → 0.3 », or « version inchangée » when the session already incremented it), following the "Professional profile status" section of `AGENTS.md`.

When the information comes from an external reference consulted online (see the "Web browsing" section of `AGENTS.md`), state its provenance (link and consultation date) in the proposal and record it in `data/profile/sources/external-references.md`.

Capitalize only information that is durable, validated, distinct, reusable and current. Do not add transient emotions, unvalidated interpretations, company-specific messaging or complete scripted answers. Add one concise history entry after an approved significant update.

Every change of the profile status or version also adds one entry to « Historique synthétique »: date, old and new status and version, reason in one line, candidate validation. One entry may cover both a significant update and the version change it causes. Use the codes in the entry, for example:

```text
- 2026-10-07 : draft 0.2 → ready 1.0. Dossier jugé suffisant pour travailler sur des opportunités. Validé par le candidat.
```

Update the header's « Version », « Statut » and « Dernière mise à jour » lines with the same change.
