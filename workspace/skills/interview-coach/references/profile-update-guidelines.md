# Controlled profile updates

Never update the profile silently.

Present:

- additions proposed;
- enrichments proposed;
- replacements proposed, with old and new wording;
- removals or archival suggestions;
- facts to confirm;
- opportunity-specific content deliberately not capitalized.

When the information comes from an external reference consulted online (see the "Web browsing" section of `AGENTS.md`), state its provenance (link and consultation date) in the proposal and record it in `data/profile/sources/external-references.md`.

Capitalize only information that is durable, validated, distinct, reusable and current. Do not add transient emotions, unvalidated interpretations, company-specific messaging or complete scripted answers. Add one concise history entry after an approved significant update.
